✦ Printing Guidelines ✦

Our fine art photography and typography prints are available as high-resolution digital downloads, giving you complete flexibility over how you display them.

📐 Best Printing Size
Our prints are rendered in a crisp 3:2 aspect ratio. This makes them a perfect fit for printing in high detail at larger scales, particularly 20" x 30" (50cm x 76cm) or smaller ratios like 4" x 6" and 12" x 18".

🖨️ How to Print Your Digital Files:
1. Local Print Shop: Save your downloaded file to a USB drive and bring it to your local office store or professional photo print shop.
2. Online Services: Upload your file to services like Shutterfly, VistaPrint, or local photo processors and select a 20x30 poster or canvas.
3. Home Printing: Smaller sizes (like 4" x 6") can be printed directly on high-quality home photo paper.

We highly recommend selecting a matte or satin finish cardstock rather than high-gloss to preserve the soft, peaceful atmosphere of the art.
